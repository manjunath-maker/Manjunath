Manju's TH5 Farming Base

Two closed wall rings with your storages and Town Hall in the middle. Mines and collectors sit outside so raiders waste time on them before they reach anything that matters.

What's hurting you right now

  • The wall is open on the left. It's a V shape with no closed loop, so ground troops walk straight in without breaking a single wall. A wall only helps if it goes all the way round.
  • Your best Archer Tower (level 6) sits outside the wall on the far left, guarding only bombs and an empty patch. It belongs inside where it can cover the storages.
  • Defenses are clumped around the Town Hall, while the elixir collectors and spare wall pieces are scattered at the edges. One spell or one group of Giants takes out most of your defense at once.

Why it works for farming

Raiders mostly go after storages. Here every storage is behind at least one wall and covered by the Mortar, the Wizard Tower and two or more Cannons and Archer Towers at the same time. Mines and collectors outside the walls act as bait, so Barbarians and Goblins spread out on them and lose time while your defenses fire. The Clan Castle in the middle means your clan's troops come out where the fight actually is. The Air Defense and the Air Bomb sit on the bottom side together to cover against Balloons and Dragons.
